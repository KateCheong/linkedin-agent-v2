POST DATE : 2026-08-14
TOPIC: AGENTIC AI - HOW IT WORKS
TAGS: #BuildInPublic #AIJourney #AIagents #FutureOfWork
Agentic AI is not magic.

Once you understand what's under the hood - it's just another user case of LLM.
This week, I built my First AI agent from scratch. It research a topic and drafts a LinkedIn Post. 
Same Python. Same OpenAI API. But agentic AI structure.
No framework, build from scratch. link in comment.
The difference - ReAct Loop:

🟣 THINK - the LLM reasons about what to do next
🔵 ACT - It selects and calls a tool (web search, calculator, file writer)
🟢 OBSERVE - It reads the result and decides: done, or loop again?

No blackbox. Just a loop - design by human.
Because here's what most people miss:
the Human IS the intellgence layer.

We write the system prompt (the agent's personality and rules)
We design the tools (what it can and cannot touch)
We set the guardrails (when it must stop and ask us)

Without human judgement - the agent is just an expensive auto completion.
As an finance professional, this matters deeply.
We are not replacing decision-maker.
We are building systems that let human work at 10x scale.

My goal is not to become an AI engineer.
It's to become an AL catalyst - someone who bridge what AI can do and what the industry needs.
If you are also curious about AI like me. DM me, happy to chat.

TOPIC: AI and handson building
TAGS: #AI #GitHub #LangChain #AIJourney #FinTech

I have built 11 AI tools in the past few weeks.

it is now on GitHub, here is what's inside - and why the way it is.
The Repository:ai-catalyst 

a learning project. Production standard. 
Not because I was using them in production. Because this is good habit to carry into production.

What Production Grade means here
➡️ API Key in env variables not hardcoded. Not committed. Not embarrassing.
➡️ Guardrails on every AI output. The AI is told explicitly what it must never say. Test by trying to break it. Repeatly.
➡️ JSON output defined schemas structured. Parseable. Ready to plug into anything. Not a paragraph of text nobody asked for.
➡️ Commit history tells a story Every change documented. Every fix explained. Future me will thank present me.

What is inside 📥 

🧷 firstcall.py : My 1st ever API call to GPT-40-mini. >100 tokens, cost basically nothing.
🧷 transaction_analyser.py: Transaction IN, Risk assessment OUT. JSON, structured. 1 if statement away from triggering a real workflow.
🧷 safe_banking_assistant.py: Refuses legal advice. refuses speculation. refuses to discuss anything off-topic. Telling AI what NOT to do.
🧷 langchain_rag.py: The capstone. answer questions from your actual docuemnts. Cites the exact policy section is used. Refuses to invent facts. in regulated world, this is not optional.

What I actually learnt
🔎 The code is not hard, know what the code must never do is the hard part.
🔎 Production grade is about discipline.

What's next
🚢 Terminal -> Browser.Script -> API.Laptop -> Deployed
🚢 Repository updates as each piece ships.
